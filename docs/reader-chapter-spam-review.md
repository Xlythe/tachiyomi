# Chapter-size filtering and review

The default reader combines saved image rules with chapter-local geometry. Original downloads and page indices are preserved.

The owned export contains 174 whole-page hides and 75 original pages with retained story content, across 125 chapters. All 249 original hashes and dimensions were verified. The export contains selected reports, not complete chapters, so it does not establish chapter-wide recall. Many adverts are 1532 by 1024, while common story-bearing sizes are 720/800/960 by 1500. Retained story originals also include 1320/1336-pixel pages and 783–1248-pixel tail fragments. Comparing every page to the maximum height would hide legitimate content.

A reference requires five eligible pages and 75% observed coverage, with a 75% majority width. Width clustering allows 1% (at least two pixels). A common height requires a 70% cluster within that width, rather than selecting the tallest page. Automatic exclusion requires BOTH a width more than 1% wider (at least two pixels) AND a height more than 15% shorter (plus a four-pixel rounding allowance). Expected-width short pages, narrower pages, and wider-only pages stay visible. Both a dominant width and a common height are required. Plausible first-page covers, double spreads, proportional resizes and the final three pages are protected from the corresponding ambiguous size decisions. Variable-height or mixed-width chapters without strong evidence stay visible. Saved whole-page hides and user crop/watermark labels retain precedence; chapter overrides are stronger for their exact content.

These protections deliberately leave some adverts visible: normal-sized spam cannot be found by geometry, and a short footer alone cannot be distinguished from legitimate chapter-tail content. The existing image matcher remains active. Unfamiliar middle-page content that is both wider and shorter can still be a false positive; use the review action to restore it.

The reader overflow menu contains **Review excluded pages**. It shows original previews, original page numbers, sizes and reasons. **Restore** and **Exclude** are chapter-specific; **Automatic** removes that override. **Edit image rule** opens the existing non-destructive editor for reusable rules. A newly saved image rule supersedes the chapter override, and restoring the original explicitly keeps that page. Overrides use chapter identity and exact encoded content hash, so they survive reopening and reevaluation without leaking to another chapter or changed image bytes.

Only bounded background header probes run for size evidence (up to 512 evenly distributed pages per loaded chapter); unfetched pages are not downloaded just to classify them. A loaded page's existing digest is checked before any automatic replacement renders. No image matching runs in the dimension probe and the visible reader does not wait for the probe. Unknown/error/interrupted evidence stays unknown. Reference preparation can complete after an unfamiliar page initially appears; this is not a promise of flash-free filtering for unknown online chapters.

Explicit overrides are atomically persisted with a 4096-choice limit and a bounded interrupted-write budget. Capacity/corruption failures preserve existing data and show an error. No sample images, URLs, titles or reading events are uploaded by this feature. Reporting remains unconfigured.

Automatic geometry decisions are derived in memory on every review; they are not persisted as image rules or overrides. Reopening with the corrected policy therefore clears exclusions caused only by the previous width-or-height heuristic. Recalculation also clears a page's old derived reason. Independent image rules and explicit KEEP/HIDE choices are preserved; no destructive data migration is needed.

## Recorded-dimension comparison (previous width-or-height policy)

A private audit could construct 128 synthetic chapter contexts using recorded story dimensions from the same source/series family. This is a proxy, not a replay of complete real chapters. It included 68 whole-page spam samples and 60 story-bearing originals: geometry excluded 9 spam samples, retained all 60 story-bearing samples, and preserved all known story labels. Of 59 spam samples not excluded by this tier, 40 had no qualifying geometric signal and 19 were protected ambiguous outliers. Another 121 samples lacked sufficient or consistent story context and were not assigned invented references. These historical results describe the previous policy, not a remeasurement of the narrower conjunctive rule. They establish the limits of the hypothesis; they are not a general recall or false-positive estimate.

## Visibility update isolation

Both reader modes scope chapter visibility notifications to the original page's own visibility revision before `collectLatest`. A decision for another page or neighbouring chapter must not cancel a pending decode or reload an unchanged visible image. Split-page holders share their original page's revision. Actual hide/restore changes still update the affected holders; rule and loading-status notifications retain their existing behavior. This change does not replace adapter items, recreate the reader, or reset its scroll position. Visual Android qualification is separate from the deterministic flow regressions.
